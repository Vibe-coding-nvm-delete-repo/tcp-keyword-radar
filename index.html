    Method and limits

    How this was built, and what it does not prove.

    Everything here comes from live pages fetched on November 2, 2026. No volume estimates were invented. No page was called clear just because a title was different.

    Steps

    1. Read the 24 converted URLs and match them to the live site index. 23 are live; Genesis Recovery is a 404.
    2. Derive one seed query per converted page, then add cluster and company seeds: 241 seeds across six clusters.
    3. Harvest Google and Bing autocomplete with alphabet, question and modifier expansions.
    4. Harvest Google PAA at click depth 4 for 22 seeds, plus Google top 10 for the same queries.
    5. Harvest Bing top 10 for 1,098 queries across every seed.
    6. Match all 11,497 keywords to 13,105 live pages with SQLite FTS5 and Porter stemming.
    7. Generate page ideas from the keyword gaps and the earlier candidate list, then judge each against the closest live pages.
